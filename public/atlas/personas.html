Who Lance is for

Five proto-personas built from the brief with the user-persona and jobs-to-be-done skills. The starter is primary: if Lance works for someone with a skill and no clients, it works for everyone after her.

پنج پرسونای اولیه که از روی بریف ساخته شدن. تازه‌کار، پرسونای اصلیه: اگه لنس برای کسی که مهارت داره ولی هنوز کارفرما نداره جواب بده، برای همه‌ی بعدی‌ها هم جواب می‌ده.

PRIMARY

Nazanin, 22 · Starter

“I can design. I just don’t know how anyone gets their first client.”

Job to be done: When I finish learning a skill, I want one real piece of work and one fair first job, so I can call myself a freelancer.

Frustrations: no feedback, no portfolio, every job asks for experience.

Design implication: free paths that end in a Showcase piece; Merlin so she doesn’t underprice.

Kaveh, 31 · Freelancer

“I lose an hour every morning checking five job sites.”

Job to be done: When good jobs appear anywhere, I want to see only the ones that fit, so I can spend my time on proposals that win.

Design implication: Job Radar with dedupe and fit, Smith and Herald for the paperwork.

Shirin, 38 · Employer

“I don’t know what a website should cost, or who to trust.”

Job to be done: When I need something built, I want a fair price and a safe way to pay, so I can hire without being taken advantage of.

Design implication: Merlin with reasons, escrow, the Agentic PMO if she can’t manage it herself.

Kian, 27 · Agent user · and Mina, 41 · Teacher

Kian: When routine work piles up, I want an agent I can watch and correct, so I can ship without hiring yet.

Mina: When I know a craft deeply, I want to package it into a course quickly, so I can earn while I sleep without becoming a full-time creator.
