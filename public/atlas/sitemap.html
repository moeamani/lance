DECISION

Why five

Hick’s law and the IA skill both cap primary navigation at 5–7 items. Five intents cover every top task in three clicks or fewer.

Agents appear twice

Each agent has its own page under Agents and appears inside the intent it serves (the Scout in Work, Merlin in Hire, the Smith in Plus).

Validation gap

No card sort or tree test yet. Run a 15-person closed card sort per language before launch.
