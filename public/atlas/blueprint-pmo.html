Agentic PMO: what the client never sees

The client sees a plan, a morning summary and a weekly call. Below the line of visibility, the Steward and a Lance project manager do the work that makes those feel effortless.

FOCAL

Burndown risk check

Every morning the Steward compares done points with the plan and flags a slipping sprint before the client feels it. It is the one backstage step the offer’s promise depends on.

Human in the loop

The PM edits agent output before the weekly call. Tiers differ only in how much human time sits below the line.

Failure to design for

If the brief is vague, every downstream cell degrades. The intake form asks for “done looks like” before anything else.
